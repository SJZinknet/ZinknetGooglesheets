"The trumpet/cornett part contains the name of the players that played this part in different seasons:
1893-96: Mlle. Armand
1897-98: Marie Brema
1909-13: Mlle. Croigu
This part seems to have been borrowed by the Concertgebouw Orkest Amsterdam, played by D. Speets and E. Kresse and Dierentuin-orkest Antwerpen, played by E. De Wolf and G. De Meyer in 1908 [19h8?]"
