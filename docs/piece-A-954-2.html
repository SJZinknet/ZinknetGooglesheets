CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
13. O ihr lieben Hirten: SSATB, 2Ctto, “trombone picciolo,” 2Trb, Tior, Bc (with symphonie for V, Va, Bc).
In the above works, the instruments always play together with the corresponding vocal parts, but cornetts and violins as well as trombones and violas play in different sections.
