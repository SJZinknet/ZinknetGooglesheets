Remarks: Most of the entries in this list end with the phrase in concto e ripni or similar. This indicates that the listed voices and instruments are considered concertante or solo parts, while the scoring additionally includes ripieni voices, usually in the same configuration as the concto voices, as well as basso continuo instrument(s), usually an organ at the very least. This can be seen, for example, by comparing the DS scoring for the Missa Novis Regis—8vv, 2Vn, 2Vtta, 4Trb in conctoe ripni—with that of the preserved exemplar in CZ-KRa: SSAATTBB conc, SSAATTBB rip, 2Vn conc, 2Vtta conc, 4Trb conc, Tior, Org. The use of basso continuo instrument(s) can be assumed even where they are not expressly indicated.
