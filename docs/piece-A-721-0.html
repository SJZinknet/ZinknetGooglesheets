Remarks: Incomplete manuscript copies formerly in the Pfarrarchiv Udestedt are preserved at D-EFu 13-M. 8° 1222 (Tenor) and D-WRha Udestedt 38a Teil 9, fol. 11r–36r (Cantus 1), Udestedt 38b Teil 8, fol. 1r–21r (Bc). In the three five-part sonatas (nos 17–19), the two upper parts switch back and forth between trombone and cornett, in at least one case with only a half-note rest in between. It therefore seems unlikely that a single player would have been expected to play both instruments.
