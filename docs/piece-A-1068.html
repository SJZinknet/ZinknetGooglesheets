A-1068 — 122 ‘Abblase-Stückgen’

Reiche, Gottfried

Instrumental
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
winds (X)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
122 ‘Abblase-Stückgen’

Remarks: In the preface to the Vier und zwantzig Quatricinia, Reiche mentions forty Sonatas à 5 that he intended to publish. These sonatas probably never appeared in print and are now lost. In addition, his estate contained a collection of 122 Abblase-Stckgen (i.e. little pieces to play from the tower) that are also no longer extant.
ComposerReiche, Gottfried
