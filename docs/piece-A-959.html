CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Kommt her zu mir: SATB, 2V, 2[Ctto], Bc.
The indication of instrumentation is unclear in this ms. Woodward interprets the headings: “V:i” and “C:i” as Violino primo and Cornetto primo respectively. The latter abbreviation could just as easily mean Corno or Clarino.
