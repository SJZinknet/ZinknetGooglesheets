CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Ms contains 120 untexted vocal pieces by various authors, 34 of which specify cornett(s). See also composers Corteccia, F., Gabrieli A., Gosswin, A., Lange, G., Lasso, O., Monte, P., Ruffo, V., Striggio, A., Utendal, A., Wert, G.

PLW, The Early Trombone :
Remarks: Concerning this manuscript, see Abran. Nos 99 and 101 are each missing one part: 103 is missing a tenor part; the bass part specifies a slightly different scoring: Bomb, 2Ctto, 3Trb. The manuscript attributes 82. Bonus est Dominus and 103. Salve rex regnum [sic] to Anonymous. No. 84 Jauchzet dem Herren is attributed to Giaches Wert in the manuscript; however, it is actually a work by Lasso, Vn jour l’amant, with a German contrafactum. Discand probably indicates a soprano shawm.
