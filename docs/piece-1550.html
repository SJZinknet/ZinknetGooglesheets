CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Sonata à 4. due violini, e due cornetti divisi in due chori: ChI 2V, Bc; ChII 2Ctto; Bc.
In the Torino ms the title appears as above and the partitura gives the following instrumentation: “P. Cho.” – “Violini,” “2o. Cho.” – “Cornetti.” In the Modena ms only the title “[Sinfonia à] 2V solo” appears.
