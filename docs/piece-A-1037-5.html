CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
5. Magnificat super Valle che lamenti: SSATTB.
The preface recommends the alternative use of violins, cornetts, and other instruments to reinforce the soprano parts. The preface also gives an alternative of cornetts and trombones, organ or regal with one soprano, or all voices.
