CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Sinfonia avanti il Gloria: Ctto o V, Trb, Bc

Gloria: SSAT conc o (SSA, Trb); SABB rip, o (V, Ctto, 2Trb); Bc.

Confitebor . . . in consilio: TTB conc; AATTB rip, Ctto o V, s (one tenor rip part says “C. a 3. voci con cornetti se piace”), Bc.

PLW, The Early Trombone :
Sinfonia avanti il Gloria: Vn/Ctto, Trb, Org

Remarks: Both editions were apparently published over several years; G3460 in 1630 (S/T, T, A, T/B, Bc) and 1632 (six partbooks with ripieno vocal and instrumental parts); G3461 in 1635 (A, T, B), 1636 (S, Org), and 1647 (rip); see also Vocal Music.
