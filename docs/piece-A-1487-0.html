Remarks: The Preface states that in nos 6–9, ChI may be performed with one voice, violins and cornetti, and ChII with one voice, trombones and other instruments. Some possible instrumentations are:
6. Herr, unser Herrscher: ChI S, 3Vn/3Ctto; ChII A, 3Trb/tbb; [Ch] cap SSATB, Bc (SWV 27)
7. Wohl dem, der nicht wandelt: ChI S, 2Vn/2Ctto, Vo; ChII A, 3Trb/ttb, Bc (SWV 28)
8. Wie lieblich sind deine Wohnungen: ChI S, 3Vn/3Ctto; ChII T, 3Trb/tbb, Bc (SWV 29)
9. Wohl dem, der den Herren fchtet: ChI S, 3Vn/3Ctto; ChII A, 3Trb/tbb, Bc (SWV 30)

In no. 17, Lobet den Herren, verses 2, 4, 6, and 8 are for two vocal soloists and solo instruments: SS, Vn, Fl, 2Bn~AA, 2Bn/2Trb~TT, 2Ctto, 2Trb~BB; verse 10 is for SSTT, and the uneven numbered verses are tutti. The cornett and trombone parts are texted (aside from some marked solo sections), so voices may be substi­tuted. A four-part sinfonia composed by Johann Crer to precede no. 17 (Vn/Fl, Trb extant only) exists as an insertion in the D-Bn source (see ‘Crüger, Johann’ above).
No. 24 has one Parte per le Trombette. à 13. Mit Trommeten und Heerpauken (part for the trumpet. à 13. With trumpets and timpani). It can thus be assumed that a trumpet ensemble with six + six trumpets and one tim­pani player was intended, and that the timpani and lower trumpet parts were improvised in the usual manner (see, for example, Daniel Speer, Grundrichtiger Unterricht [Ulm, 1697], pp. 208–13 and 219–21).
