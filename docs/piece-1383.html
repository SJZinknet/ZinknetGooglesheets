1383 — Per questi duo guerrieri famosi

Rossetto, Stefano

Vocal / Mixed
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
V (6), fl traversa (1), cnto (1), trb (2), lira da braccio (1), lute (2), cemb (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Per questi duo guerrieri famosi: 6vv, traverso, Ctto, 2Trb, lira da braccio, 2Lt, Cemb

Remarks: Canzonas performed in Florence in 1567 on a float in a parade in honour of the baptism of Leonora, the daughter of Grand Duke Francesco de’ Medici and Johann of Austria, described in CeccherelliD.
ComposerRossetto, Stefano
Bibliography:
PEARSE, Linda ; LEONARD, Charlotte A. ; WEINER, Howard T, The Early Trombone : a Catalog of Music. Turnhout: Brepols, 2023. (Vocal Music)
