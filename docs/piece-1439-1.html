Balletti à 2 Chori: ChI 2Vn/2Ctto, 2Va/2Trb; ChII 2Tpt; b

Remarks: Preserved anonymously; SehnalC attributes this piece to Schmelzer. According to SehnalC, the violin parts were originally marked Cornetto o Violino, and the viola parts Trombone o Viola.
