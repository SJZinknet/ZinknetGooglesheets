INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
The following pieces are found in the list of Ferdinand’s works in the Distinta Specificatione (DS), the catalogue of the private library of Emperor Leopold I, fols 1r–5r:

Magnificat à 20: 6vv, 2Vn, 4Va, 2Tpt conc&rip, 2Ctto, 4Trb
Placare Christe Servulis: 4vv, 4Vn, 3Va, Bn rip,2Ctto, 4Trb
Requiem per l’Augustissima Imperatrice Margarita à 15: SSATB, 5Vtta, 2Ctto muto, 3Trb conc&rip

Remarks: Most of the entries in this list end with the phrase conctocon ripni or similar. This indicates that the listed voices and instruments are considered concertante or solo parts, while the scoring additionally includes ripieni voices, usually in the same configuration as the concto voices, as well as basso continuo instrument(s), usually an organ at the very least.
