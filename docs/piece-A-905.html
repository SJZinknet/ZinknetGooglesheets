Instrumentation from catalogues
CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Act 3 scene VII, Plutone, Proserpina, Aletto, Choro di Mostri:
Ritornello “Cornetti e Tromboni”: 2Ctto, 2Trb (followed by “Terribile horribile” a 3: ATB).
Act 3 scene XI, Ritornello “Cornetti”: 2Ctto, 2Trb (preceded by a trio “Si si si savventura”: Plutone, Radamanto, and Minosso.
Act 3 scene XIII, Ritornello “Cornetti e Tromboni”: 2Ctto, 2Trb (followed by the “Choro di Mostri”: ATB, then a repeat of the sinfonia).
Act 5 Balletto: “violini” 2V, [Va, Vc]; 2Ctto, 2Trb.
Act 5 [Balletto]: “violini” 2V, [Va, Vc]; 2Ctto, 2Trb.
Act 5 [Balletto]: “violini e cornetti insieme” 2V, [Va, Vc]; 3Ctto, Trb.
Act 5 Vaghe sfauillano (with ritornelli): SATB, tutti instruments 2V, [Va, Vc]; 2Ctto, 2Trb.

PLW, The Early Trombone :
Atto Terzo, Scena VII Terribili, horribili spiriti: ATB Coro di Mostri [Infernali], Bc; with ritornelli for 2Ctto, 2Trb, Bc
Atto Terzo, Scena XI Si si si savventura: ATB Radamanto, Minosso, Plutone, Bc; with ritornelli for 2Ctto, 2Trb, Bc
Atto Terzo, Scena XIII Hor mentre scherzano: ATB Coro di Mostri [Infernali], Bc; with ritornelli for 2Ctto, 2Trb
Atto quinto, Scena XII, Balletto Lieto il Ciel: SATB, with interludes/ritornelli for 2Vn, [Va, Vc,] 2Ctto, 2Trb, Bc, and the last 3 verses tutti insieme

Remarks: The opera Ercole in Tebe was premiered in Florence on 8 July 1661. Chigi Q.IV.59 and Rés. Vm4.11 are largely identical, although the latter specifies only Cornetti where the former indicates Cornetti e Tromboni.
