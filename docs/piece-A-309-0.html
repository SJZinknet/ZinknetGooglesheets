CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Deus in adjiutorium meum intende à 7, 12: SSATB conc & rip, (ripieni may be replaced by 2Ctto and 3Trb), 2V, 2Cttino, 2Va, b Va, Bomb grosso.
Siehe, es hat überwunden à 14, 18: SSATTB conc, SATB rip, 2V, 2Cttino, 2Tpt, 3Trb, b Va, Bomb grosso.

3. Deus in Adjutorium meum intende à 7 vel 12: ChI SATTB; ChII SATTB rip, doubled by Ctto, 4Trb; 2V, doubled by 2Cttino ad plac; bVa ad plac, doubled by Bomb; Fag; Org.

Missa à 12: SATTB conc, 2V, 4Trb, SATTB rip (S of rip may be doubled by Ctto), 2Cttino, 2Va, Basso Va, Bomb, Fag, Org.

Magnificat à 9 vel 13: SATB conc & rip, 2V, 2Cttino ad plac, 3Trb, b Va, Bomb o Fag, Org ad plac (S rip: voce o Ctto).
Missa Cerviensiana à 12 vel 18: SSATTB conc & rip, 2V, 2Cttino, 2Va, 4Trb, b Va, Vo, Org (SS rip may be doubled with 2Ctto).
Missa Triumphalis à 14: ChI&II SATB; 2V, 2Va, 2Cttino, 4Trb, Org ad plac.

Missa concertata la lombardesca à 13: ChI&II SATB; 2V, 2Va, 2Cttino, 3Trb, bVa, Vo, Bomb, Org.
Missa à 15: ChI&II SATB; 2V, 2Cttino, 3Trb; 2Vtta, bVa [ie. 3VaG] ad plac, Vo, Bomb, Fag, Org.

4. Domine ad adjuvandum: SATTB conc; SATTB rip, Ctto, 3Trb; 2Cttino, 2V, bVa, Org.
In the cornett part and one of the trombone parts, the indications voce ó cornetto and voce ó trombone are found.
4. Magnificat anima mea Dominum à 9 vel 13, 4. voci e 5. Instrom. con 4 Rip.: SATB conc & rip (SATB o Ctto, 3Trb), 2V, 2Vtta, Vo, Cttino (doubling V1 ad plac), Ctto (doubling V2 ad plac), Va b ad plac (doubling Vo), Bomb e Fag ad plac (doubling Vo), Bc per l'organo.
6. Dixit Dominus Domino veo à 9 vel 13: SATB conc & rip; 2V, 2Va, 2Cttino; Ctto, Bomb o Fag, Vo ad plac, Bc (SB ripieno may be doubled by Ctto, Fag).
