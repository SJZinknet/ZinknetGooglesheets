1030/1 — Ref to : Laudate pueri Dominum à 7: ChI SSA; ChII ATTB
Praetorius, Michael
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
Coro 1: S (2), A (1)
Coro 2: A (1), T (2), B (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Laudate pueri Dominum à 7: ChI SSA; ChII ATTB

Remarks: Michael Praetorius, in Syntagma Musicum III, pp. 153–54, demonstrates possible instrumental scorings using the above motets by Lasso as examples.

For Laudate pueri Dominum, Praetorius suggests that the two soprano parts of the first choir be taken by two transverse flutes or two descant violins or two cornetts, with the alto part being sung; in the second choir, the alto part is to be sung, and the two tenor parts and the bass played by trombones: ChI 2Vn/2Fl/2Ctto, A; ChII A, 3Trb. Laudate pueri is also found in D-Rp A.R. 775–777 with the instrumentation 2Bomb, 2Ctto, 3Trb (see Lasso in Instrumental Music).

1030/2 — Ref to : In convertendo Dominus à 8: ChI SAAT; ChII SATB
Praetorius, Michael
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
ChI {S (1), A (2), T (1)}, ChII {S (1), A (1), T (1), B (1)}
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION (ALTERNATIVE)
Coro 1: S (1), T (1), vl/fl fl traversa/recorder/cnto muto (3), cnto (1), trb ad lib/fag (1)
Coro 2: S (1), A (1), T (1), B (1), vla da gamba/b-vla/recorder (1), fag/trb (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
In convertendo Dominus à 8: ChI SAAT; ChII SATB

Remarks: Michael Praetorius, in Syntagma Musicum III, pp. 153–54, demonstrates possible instrumental scorings using the above motets by Lasso as examples.

For In convertendo Dominus, Praetorius suggests in ChI three flutes or three mute cornetts or three violins, or alternatively violin, cornett and transverse flute or recorder, and a tenor possibly doubled by a trombone, and possibly a choirboy on the soprano part so that the text can be heard; in ChII solo voices or viola da gamba or viola da braccio or recorder, a bassoon or quart-trombone, and either soprano and/or tenor voices: ChI S, 3Vn/3Fl/3Ctto muto~Vn, Fl (Trav/Rec), Ctto, T&Trb ad lib~Trb/Bn; ChII SATB~S/T~ST, VaG/VaB/ Rec, Bn/Trb.

1030/3 — Ref to : Quo properas à 10: ChI SATTB; ChII SATTB
Praetorius, Michael
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
Coro 1: S (1), A (1), T (2), B (1)
Coro 2: S (1), A (1), T (2), B (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Quo properas à 10: ChI SATTB; ChII SATTB

Remarks: Michael Praetorius, in Syntagma Musicum III, pp. 153–54, demonstrates possible instrumental scorings using the above motets by Lasso as examples.

For Quo properas he gives seven variants, all but one with trombones, also suggesting that a soprano, alto, or tenor voice sing the text in each choir: 1. ChI S/Ctto, 4Trb; ChII S/Ctto, 3Trb, Trb majore 2. ChI SATTB; ChII Ctto, 4Trb 3. ChI SATTB; ChII [5]VaB 4. ChI SATTB; ChII 2Fl, Bn, 2Trb 5. ChI [5]VaB; ChII Fif, 4Trb 6. ChI [5]VaB; ChII Fif, Fl, Bn, 2Trb 7. ChI 2Fl, Bn, 2Trb; ChII Ctto, 4Trb
