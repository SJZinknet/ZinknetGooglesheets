A-487 — Cacciator Donne scorriamo

Corteccia, Francesco

Vocal / Mixed
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
V (6), crummhorn (2), cnto (2), trb (2)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Cacciator Donne scorriamo: 6vv, 2storte,2Ctto, 2Trb

Remarks: Canzonas performed in Florence in 1567 in honour of the baptism of Leonora, the daughter of Grand Duke Francesco de’Medici and Johann of Austria, described by CeccherelliD.
ComposerCorteccia, Francesco
