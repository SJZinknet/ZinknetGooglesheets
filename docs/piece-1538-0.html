Remarks: The AII and pars 7 and 8 pro instrumentis partbooks are no longer extant, which means that a number of pieces in this collection are incomplete. In most cases, it was possible to determine the designation of the missing parts from the indications in the title headings of the extant parts or from the context. In the cases of two vocal works, nos 32 and 42, the missing instrumental part may or may not have been intended for Trb or Va/Trb, respectively. The Bn/Trb part of no. 21 is texted. In no. 31, the trombone part is texted, and the B/Trb ad lib part specifies neither‘and’ nor‘or’, and thus might properly be B&Trb ad lib. No. 39, Domini est terra, was preserved with an attribution to Schtz, arranged and supplemented by five ad­ditional bassoon parts, in the now lost Ms Gotthold 13609(3) of the Knigliche-und Universitäts-Bibliothek Knigsberg; it was published by Philipp Spitta in 1893 in vol. 13 of the Sämtliche Werke of Heinrich Schtz, and subsequently assigned the number 476 in the catalogue of Schütz’s works (SWV); it is also listed in the Ansbach inventory of 1686. No. 33 is also listed in an inventory of the St. Michaelisschule in Leburg (c. 1695). See also Instrumental Music.
