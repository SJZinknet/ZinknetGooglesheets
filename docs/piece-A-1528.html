PLW, The Early Trombone :
Te Deum laudamus:ChI 4vv,2Vn,Vo, Zwerchpfeiff,2Lt, Org; ChII 4vv,Bn, Ctto, 2Trb, Regal; ChIII 4vv, 3Trb, Serpentin, Regal
Remarks: According to AssumW, a Te Deum laudamus by Salomon was performed in the church for the baptism of Prince Friedrich von Wrttemberg on 10 March 1616 in Stuttgart. When the three choirs performed together, they were additionally joined by the large organ, a cornett, and a großen Pommerten Vagoten (i.e. contrabass-oon).The Zwerchpfeiff is a transverse flute.
