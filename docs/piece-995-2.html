995/2 — Gaudeamus omnes in Domino. Motetto a 8 con Trombe, Cornetti e Violini

Perti, Giacomo Antonio

Vocal / MixedMs.
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
Coro 1 conc: S (1), A (1), T (1), B (1)
Coro 2 cap: S (1), A (1), T (1), B (1)
tr (2), cnto/ob (2), vl (2), violetta (1), vlc (1), org (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Gaudeamus omnes in Domino. Motetto a 8 con Trombe, Cornetti e Violini: Ch I&II SATB conc & cap, 2Tpt, 2Ctto (o 2Ob), 2V, Vtta, Vc “spezzato,” Org.

The indication Cornetti in the score has been crossed out and replaced with Obuis by a later hand. The corresponding parts (in a different hand) are labelled Tromba 3a and 4a, while all of the other parts (in yet another hand) carry the heading “Motetto con trombe, et Obuè e V.V.”
