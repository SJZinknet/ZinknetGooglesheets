Laudate Dominum: SATB, SATB, 2Bn, 2Ctto, 4Trb

Source:
Lost. Possibly in Aichinger, Liber Sacrarvm (1597; RISM A/AA519)

Remarks: According to Johann August Assum, Warhaffte Relation und historischer, politischer, höfflicher Discours über
[…] Johann Friderichen, Hertzogen zu Würtemberg […] jungen Sohns Printz Friderichen angestelter […]
Kind-Tauff (1616), a Laudate Dominum by Aichinger was performed in the church for the baptism of Prince
Friedrich von Württemberg on 10 March 1616 in Stuttgart. The Laudate Dominum from Aichinger’s Liber
Sacrarvm is without instruments.
