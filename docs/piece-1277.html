1277 — Missa S. Venantii [C]

Reutter, Georg

Vocal / MixedMs.
UNIFORM INSTRUMENTATION
S (1), A (1), T (1), B (1), vl (2), vlc (1), vlne (1), fag conc (1), tr (4), trb (4), timp (1), org conc (1)
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Missa S. Venantii [C]: SATB, 2Vn, Vc, Vo, Bn conc, 4Tpt, 4Trb, Timp, Org conc (Hofer 30/56, 31/57)
ComposerReutter, Georg
Bibliography:
PEARSE, Linda ; LEONARD, Charlotte A. ; WEINER, Howard T, The Early Trombone : a Catalog of Music. Turnhout: Brepols, 2023. (Vocal Music)
Note:
Found in PEARSE, Linda: The Early Trombone : a Catalog of Music. Cornett is not mentioned in the instrumentation of the piece, but one of the sources is marked "without" cornett, implying that cornett should be part of the instrumentation in the other sources. To be verified.
