PLW, The Early Trombone :
Nur in meines Jesu Wunden: SSATTB, 5VaG, Vo, 2Cttino, 3Trb, Bc
Remarks: In S-Uu vmhs 32:11, the Viola 1 and 2 parts indicate a change to Cornet after the opening sonata, as do Violas 3–5 to Tromb, although the latter three parts eventually switch back to Viol. Also listed in an inventory of the St. Michaelisschule in Leburg (c. 1695) and in the Ansbach inventory of 1686.
