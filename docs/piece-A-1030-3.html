PLW, The Early Trombone :
Quo properas à 10: ChI SATTB; ChII SATTB

Remarks: Michael Praetorius, in Syntagma Musicum III, pp. 153–54, demonstrates possible instrumental scorings using the above motets by Lasso as examples.

For Quo properas he gives seven variants, all but one with trombones, also suggesting that a soprano, alto, or tenor voice sing the text in each choir: 1. ChI S/Ctto, 4Trb; ChII S/Ctto, 3Trb, Trb majore 2. ChI SATTB; ChII Ctto, 4Trb 3. ChI SATTB; ChII [5]VaB 4. ChI SATTB; ChII 2Fl, Bn, 2Trb 5. ChI [5]VaB; ChII Fif, 4Trb 6. ChI [5]VaB; ChII Fif, Fl, Bn, 2Trb 7. ChI 2Fl, Bn, 2Trb; ChII Ctto, 4Trb
