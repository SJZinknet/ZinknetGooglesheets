Missa IV: S, SATB rip 2Vn, 3Va/3Trb ad lib, Org Sinfonia ad lib: 2Vn, 3Va/3Trb, Org

Missa VI Requiem: SATB, SAAT rip 2Vn, 3Trb rip, Org Sinfonia before Kyrie: 2Vn, 3Trb, Org Sinfonia after Kyrie: 2Vn, 3Trb, Org Sinfonia before Sanctus: 2Vn, 3Trb, Org

Te Deum laudamus: SAT, SAAT ad lib, 2Vn, 3Trb ad lib

Remarks: In Missa II and Missa VI, the trombones have shared parts with voices, indicated Trombone & Voce ad libitum and Voce & Trombo.ad libitum respectively. Both works display markings specifying tromb.solo (trombone alone) and tutti (trombone and voice together). In the Te Deum, part titles designate Voce & Instromento ad libitum, however, trombones are specified in the parts, with indications of Tromb., appearing in the same manner as in the other works.
