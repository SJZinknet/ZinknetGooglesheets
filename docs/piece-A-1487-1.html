CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
6. Herr unser Herrscher [SWV 27]: ChI SSAT o (1v, 4Ctto) o (2V, 2Va); ChII ATBB o (1v, 4Trb) o other; Capella SATB ad lib, Vo, Org.
