In "distribution de l'orchestre" d'Indy writes: "Cornet à bouquin en UT (dans la coulisse) | donnant les notes réelles: [F4 clef G3 and Bb3] note : cette partie peut être jouée par l'un des exécutants chargés des parties de Saxhorns."
Cornetto in C (backstage) | giving the sounding [F4 clef G3 and Bb3] note : this part can be played by one of the players that plays the Saxhorn parts.
The cornett plays offstage in Act II. At the beginning of the system is written "cor anglais | (cornet à bouquin)". The printed version of this opera only mentions cor anglais.
