CD, A Catalog of Music for the Cornett :
Pavana dell'Ottone Landgravio H[essiae] a5 stromenti diversi, cioè Violino et viola soprano [sic], cornetto et cornetto muto, trombone: s, s, a, t, b.
The ms in which these pieces survive was copied at Kassel in 1887 by F. Liebling, oboist of the 83rd Regiment. The pieces were either composed or arranged after 1615 by Moritz Landgraf von Hessen.
