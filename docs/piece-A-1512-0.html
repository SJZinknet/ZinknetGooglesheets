Remarks: Selle suggests a number of possible scorings (including octave transpositions of various parts) for the pieces in this collection. The basic formation for all the pieces is solo voice(s) and basso continuo. Listed above are Selle’s suggestions with trombone(s) found in the Nota at the end of the Vox I partbook and in the preface of the Vox Tertia pro Capella Fidicinia partbook. In cases where multiple scorings with trombone are suggested, they are separated by a tilde [~]. In addition, the continuo parts can be played by a harmony instrument (organ, regal, harpsichord, spinet, theorbo, lute) and/or melody instrument (violone, trombone, or bassoon), or even be sung. The pieces of this collection are also preserved in Selle’s manuscript Opera omnia, sometimes with yet other scorings (see below).
