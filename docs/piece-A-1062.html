PLW, The Early Trombone :
Seraphim clamabant: ChI SAT, B&Vo/Bn/Trb; ChII ATT; ChIII S&Vn/Ctto, S&Ctto, T&Trb

Remarks: Seraphim clamabant was written in honour of the investiture of Peter Samuel Theodoricus (1580–1640) as rector of the University of Jena in 1620. The partbook designations given in RISM do not reflect the part specifications in the source; the set of parts in D-Rp is most likely complete.
