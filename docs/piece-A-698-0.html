PLW, The Early Trombone :
Act 1, scene 1, Ah, se intorno: T, SATB, 2Vn, Va, Vo, Ctto, 3Trb, Bc
Act 2, scene 1, Deh, placatevi con me: T, SATB, 2Vn, Va, Vo, Ob, 2Ctto, 2Trb, Hp, Bc

Remarks: Premiered in Vienna on 5 October 1762. In Deh, placatevi the score is ambiguous concerning the scoring of the trombones: the use of either two or three trombones is conceivable.
