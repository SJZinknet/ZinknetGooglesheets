B-3/2 — Canzon duplicata in Echo duodecimi toni a 10

Gabrieli, Giovanni

InstrumentalMs.1 concordance
ComposerGabrieli, Giovanni
LibraryD-Kl
Shelfmark2° Ms. Mus. 59r
Note:
The musical content of this piece is identical to Canzon in Echo duodecimi toni a 10 (B-3/1), but is conceived for two soprano instruments (according to the 1597 print of Sacrae Symphoniae, two cornetts), with organ, including ripieno-like sections with all 10 instruments.
