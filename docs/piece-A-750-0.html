A-750/0 — Oliva in domo dei fructifera

Hochreiter, Joseph Balthasar

Vocal / MixedPrintRISM Online
INSTRUMENTATION (CATALOGUES)
PLW, The Early Trombone :
Vesperae solemnes de Dominica: SATB conc, S/Ctto rip, A/Trb rip, T/Trb rip, B/Trb rip, 2Vn conc, 2Va conc ad lib, Vo, Org ( JBH 1/1)

Vesperae breves de Dominica: SATB conc, S/Ctto rip, A/Trb rip, T/Trb rip, B/Trb rip, 2Vn conc ad lib, 2Va conc ad lib, Vo, Org ( JBH 1/2)

8 Psalms: SATB conc, S/Ctto rip, A/Trb rip, T/Trb rip, B/Trb rip, 2Vn conc ad lib, 2Va conc ad lib, Vo, Org ( JBH 1/3)

[Credidi
In convertendo
Domine probasti me
De profundis clamavi
Memento Domine David
Beati omnes
Confitebor
In exitu Israe]

Remarks: The Vesperae solemnes de Dominica and Vesperae breves de Dominica both consist of the movements Dixit Do-minus, Confitebor, Beatus vir, Laudate pueri, Laudate Dominum, and Magnificat. The 8 Psalms are Credidi propter, In convertendo, Domine probasti me, De profundis, Memento Domine, Beati omnes, Confitebor tibi, and In exitu Israel.
ComposerHochreiter, Joseph Balthasar
